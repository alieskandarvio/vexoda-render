Gold looks random.
It climbs… then it crashes.
But every move has a cause.
It starts simple: supply and demand.
But one decision from the Fed can move the price hard.
And the dollar? When it gets stronger, gold usually gets weaker.
And when markets shake, wars and inflation, people run to gold.
So every move is a chain of causes:
the dollar and rates, fear and crises, and demand.
